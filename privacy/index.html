Fledgy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Fledgy is an app where children earn screen time by doing jobs, and look after a bird that grows as they go. This page explains, in plain words, what Fledgy stores about your family, who can see it, and how to delete it. Fledgy is made by Jake Watts ("we").

The short version
  • For your child we store only a first name, their bird's name and their timezone.
  • Photos of finished jobs are deleted 7 days after you answer them.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling. Nothing is shared except with the services that run Fledgy.
  • Only your family can see your family's data.
  • You can delete everything at any time: Parent mode → Settings → Delete my family.

What we store

WhatWhy
Parent: your Google account's email address and the basic profile Google shares (name and picture), a PIN (stored only as a scrambled hash, never the PIN itself), and when you agreed to these terms To sign you in, keep Parent mode locked, send you an email when your child finishes a job, and record your consent
Child: first name, the bird's name, timezone To greet your child, name their bird, and reset screen time at their midnight

Screen time: when the countdown is started, paused and finished each day To show how much time is left
Photos of finished jobs (only for jobs you mark "Needs a photo") So you can see the job was done. Deleted 7 days after you approve or say Not yet
The bird: its progress and what it's wearing For the bird's journey and dress-up
Paired devices: an anonymous sign-in for your child's device, the device type (for example "iPhone") and when it was paired; wrong pairing codes tried in the last hour To link your child's device to your family, and to stop people guessing codes

We do not collect your child's surname, age, birthday, location, contacts, or anything from other apps. Fledgy has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Who can see it

Only the parent who set up the family and the devices you pair to your child. Each family's data is kept apart by security rules on our servers: no other family, and no other device, can see or change it.

Services that run Fledgy

We use a small number of companies to run Fledgy. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: stores Fledgy's data and photos, and handles sign-in. Its servers are in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Google: Google sign-in for parents.
  • Resend: sends the "your child finished a job" emails.
  • Apple: delivers the app, and (if you allow them) notifications and the screen-time alarm on your device.

We never sell your data or share it for advertising.

Children's privacy

Fledgy is used by children, but it is set up and controlled by a parent. A parent must agree to this policy in the app before a child is added. The child's device only ever signs in anonymously, linked by a one-time code from the parent. Children cannot create accounts, buy anything, or contact anyone through Fledgy.

How long we keep it

  • Photos of finished jobs: 7 days after you answer them (and no more than 7 days after they were sent, if never answered).
  • Everything else: for as long as your family uses Fledgy. Delete your family, and it's all removed straight away.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: Parent mode → Settings → Delete my family. This removes your child, jobs, history, photos, the bird, paired devices and your sign-in record.
  • Unlink a device: Parent mode → Settings → your child's devices → Unlink.
  • Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how it's used. Email us at the address below and we'll reply within one month.
  • If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Parent mode is protected by a PIN, and changing the PIN or deleting the family needs a fresh Google sign-in. Photos are stored privately and can only be seen by your family.

Changes

If we change what Fledgy stores, we'll update this page and ask you to agree again in the app.
