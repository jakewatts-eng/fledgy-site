How Fledgy works

  1. Choose and name a bird

    Your child picks the blue bird or the pink bird and gives it a name. Most call it Pip.

  2. Go on an adventure

    Each day there's an adventure to do away from the screen. A parent ticks it off.

  3. Watch Pip grow

    Pip hatches, grows and travels to new places. A quick look in the app, then back to playing.
